04 / FRESHWATER HABITAT

Glacial Stream Dynamics

Glacial waters are characterized by low temperatures and fast currents. Rainbow Trout adapt by positioning themselves behind submerged structures to conserve energy while waiting for prey to drift downstream.

Their beautiful pink lateral line is a sensory system called the lateral line, detecting minor pressure variations in the streaming current.

08 / DWELLERS

Nature's Symbiosis

The Clownfish is coated in a special protective mucus layer that prevents it from triggering the stinging nematocysts of sea anemones. In return for protection, the fish cleans the anemone and drives away intruders.

Recreating this balance in a home aquarium requires deep engineering of organic live rock setups and customized wave pumps.
